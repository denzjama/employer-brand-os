## Purpose
Builds and maintains the editorial calendar across channels: what goes out, when, for which audience, carrying which EVP pillar, with which proof. Shows where the plan is unbalanced: one pillar overused, an audience ignored, a proof point never told.

## Fits when
- The team publishes regularly across several channels.
- The plan lives in a spreadsheet nobody fully trusts, or in someone's head.

## Triggers
Monthly planning ritual; on request when priorities change.

## Inputs
`eb/brand-core/evp.md`, `audiences.md`; the current plan (spreadsheet or file in `eb/inbox/`); the team's goals from `eb/diagnosis/`; outputs of the idea hub and social performance review.

## Outputs
`eb/work/content-planning/<month>-plan.md`: a calendar draft and a balance check by pillar, audience and channel.

## Human checkpoint
The team approves the plan at the planning meeting.

## Suggested ritual
Monthly content planning meeting: the draft is the starting point, not a blank page.

## Metrics
Plan prepared before the meeting; share of planned items delivered; pillar and audience balance.

## Risks and limits
Does not set targets for the team; proposes. Does not schedule or publish anything.
