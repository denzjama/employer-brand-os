## Purpose
A draft plan for next month, with the balance by pillar, audience and channel, ready before the planning meeting.

## Fits when
- The team publishes regularly in several channels and plans monthly.
- The plan lives in a spreadsheet nobody fully trusts, or in someone's head.

## Trigger
A few days before the monthly planning meeting.

## Steps

| From | To | What |
|---|---|---|
| lead | coordinator | Month, goals, committed dates |
| coordinator | content-planning | Task in the handoff format |
| brand-core | content-planning | EVP pillars, audiences, confirmed facts |
| content-planning | lead | Draft plan, balance and gaps |
| lead | log | Plan decided at the meeting |

## Decision
The team decides the plan at the meeting; the EB lead records the decision.

## Variants
- With an idea hub or social performance review in the team, their outputs are inputs to the plan.
- The plan owner may be a content manager instead of the lead.

## Risks and limits
The plan proposes; it assigns no work and sets no targets. Items without confirmed facts are flagged so nobody writes them as fact.
